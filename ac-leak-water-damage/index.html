Where Air Conditioner Water Comes From

An air conditioner pulls humidity out of the air, and that water has to drain away. When the drain line clogs or the pan cracks, the water goes into the building instead, often into a ceiling, a closet or an attic floor.

Turn the system off at the thermostat as soon as you see water, since running it keeps making more. Call us if materials are wet, and call an HVAC technician to clear or repair the line.

Why AC Leaks Stay Hidden

Air handlers live in attics, closets and mechanical rooms, so the first sign is often a ceiling stain in a room below. By then the insulation above has been soaking for a while.

Condensate water is not sewage, but it can sit in insulation and drywall and support mold. Technicians use moisture meters and thermal imaging to find the extent before opening a ceiling.

How the Crew Dries an AC Leak

The sequence is simple. The HVAC technician stops the leak, the crew extracts any standing water and opens wet ceiling or wall sections as needed, and equipment dries the structure.

Wet insulation is removed, because it keeps moisture against wood and drywall. We then place dehumidifiers and air movers and record readings until the structure is dry. If a ceiling opening is needed, our ceiling water damage page explains the repair stage, and mold concerns lead to mold remediation.

Preventing a Repeat

HVAC technicians can add a float switch that shuts the system off when the pan fills. They can also clear drain lines on a schedule. Those are HVAC services, and we do not provide them, but we can tell you what we found during moisture mapping.

Insurance and AC Leaks

Coverage depends on whether the leak was sudden or gradual. A clog that overflowed suddenly may be covered, while a slow drip over months may be excluded as maintenance. Your insurer decides.

Photograph the stain, the unit and the clogged line. Keep any parts the HVAC technician removes and save the invoice, since adjusters often ask for it.

The Standard Behind the Drying

Drying after a condensate leak follows ANSI/IICRC S500, the reference standard for professional water damage restoration. Under it, moisture readings rather than how a ceiling looks decide when equipment can leave.

Safety Steps for an AC Leak

Do these first

  • ✓Turn the system off at the thermostat before touching the unit or the drain pan.
  • ✓Keep clear of ceilings that sag or drip, and avoid fixtures under a wet ceiling.
  • ✓Do not open an attic hatch if the ceiling below is bulging with water.

Air Conditioning in a Lake Michigan Summer

Lake County's July mean temperature sits near 82 degrees Fahrenheit, and shoreline humidity keeps systems working hard. Heavy use produces more condensate, so drain lines that were fine in spring can clog by midsummer.
