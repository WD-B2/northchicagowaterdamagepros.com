Emergency Frozen Pipe Response: What To Do Before Help Arrives

We take frozen pipe calls 24/7, and winter cold snaps are when the line is busiest. If a pipe is frozen but not leaking, open the faucet it serves slightly, keep the room warm and call. If water is already flowing, shut the main valve and call right away.

Safe thaw and shutoff steps

  • ✓Close the main shutoff if you see any leak, spray or sagging ceiling.
  • ✓Never use an open flame, torch or propane heater on a frozen pipe.
  • ✓Warm the room and the pipe with a hair dryer or warm towels, starting nearest the faucet and working toward the frozen section.
  • ✓Leave the faucet slightly open while thawing so melting water has somewhere to go.

How a Freeze Turns Into a Flood

Ice expands as water freezes, and the pressure usually splits the pipe along its length or pops a fitting. The split often goes unnoticed because the ice plugs it, and the real flow starts when temperatures rise.

That delay is why a house can look fine at night and have an active leak by midday. The failure point is frequently in a wall, a crawl space or an unheated garage, so water can run for some time before a stain appears.

Exterior walls

Lines on the cold side of insulation freeze first, particularly near hose bibs and kitchen sinks.

Garages and crawl spaces

Unconditioned spaces drop to outdoor temperatures during a cold snap. See crawl space water damage for what follows.

Vacant units

Heat turned down in an empty home or apartment is a common setup for a split pipe. Landlords should check vacant units after a hard freeze.

Fire sprinkler and boiler lines

Commercial and multi-unit buildings can have larger lines that release more water when they fail.

What the Crew Does After a Thaw Leak

The crew starts with extraction of standing water, then checks the pipe path, the wall below it and the room underneath with moisture meters. Frozen pipe losses often have more concealed moisture than the visible wet spot suggests.

Wet insulation and drywall are removed where drying cannot reach them. Air movers and dehumidifiers then dry the remaining framing and subfloor, and technicians record daily readings. The plumber repairs the pipe, and our burst pipe page covers how those two jobs are sequenced.

Insurance and Frozen Pipes

Many homeowners policies cover water damage from a pipe that froze and burst, sometimes with conditions about keeping the home heated. Vacant property and long absences are the situations where coverage questions come up most often.

Document the split pipe, the wet areas and any temperature records you have. Keep receipts for emergency steps and ask your insurer before discarding materials.

Standards Behind the Thaw Response

After a freeze, the cleanup follows ANSI/IICRC S500, the standard reference for water damage restoration, which covers extraction, drying goals and monitoring. Cold weather lengthens drying, and the standard's measurement approach is how we tell when it is finished.
