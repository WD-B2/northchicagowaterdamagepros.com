Emergency Water Heater Leak: What To Do Before the Crew Arrives

We answer water heater leak calls 24/7. While you wait, shut off the cold water supply to the tank and switch off its power, because a gas heater and an electric heater each have a safe way to stop.

Safe shutdown

  • ✓Close the cold water inlet valve above the tank, usually a handle or wheel on the pipe.
  • ✓For electric heaters, switch off the breaker if you can reach it dry. For gas, set the gas control to off or pilot.
  • ✓Do not touch the tank or the wiring while standing in water.
  • ✓If the hot water relief valve is discharging, call a plumber while you call us. Do not cap the valve.

Where Water Heaters Fail

Tank corrosion

Steel tanks rust from the inside over time, and a failure usually shows as water pooling at the base.

Relief valve discharge

A valve that opens from excess pressure or temperature can drip steadily or release a burst of hot water.

Fittings and flex lines

Connections on the top of the heater can loosen, corrode or crack and spray along the wall.

Drain valve and sediment

Leaks at the drain valve are common and usually small, but they can run for days unnoticed.

Tank failures behave differently from slow drips. A slow leak at a fitting wets a small area for a long time, while a tank failure floods quickly and often reaches adjoining rooms or finished space.

Cleanup After a Water Heater Leak

Heaters often sit in basements, closets and utility rooms with drywall, carpet or stored boxes nearby. The crew extracts standing water, checks surrounding walls with meters and removes saturated materials.

Hot water adds one wrinkle. Warm, humid air from a hot leak holds moisture in the room, so dehumidifiers matter more than usual. Our structural drying process covers the equipment and monitoring.

Insurance and Water Heater Leaks

Sudden tank failures are commonly covered for the resulting water damage, while the cost of the heater itself is often not. Gradual leaks and maintenance failures are where claims get denied. Check your policy language or ask your agent.

Photograph the tank, the label with its age and the water damage. Keep the failed part if the plumber removes it, since an adjuster may ask to see it.

Reference Standard for Utility Room Drying

Cleanup after a heater leak follows ANSI/IICRC S500, the standard reference for professional water damage restoration. Hot water changes the humidity picture, and the standard's measurement approach is how we confirm the room is dry.
