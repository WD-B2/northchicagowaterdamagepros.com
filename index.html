Every job follows the same four steps, whether it is a small leak or a flooded basement.

1

Call the line

2

Insurance

Insurance Notes for Water Damage Claims

We Document the Loss. Your Insurer Decides Coverage.

We photograph the damage, record moisture readings and write a scope of work so your adjuster can review what happened. We do not decide coverage, and your policy and your insurer do.

The Illinois Department of Insurance advises photographing damage, reporting promptly and keeping receipts. It also notes that flood is generally not part of a standard homeowners policy and that sewer backup is covered only with added coverage.

Water Damage in North Chicago: What the Local Conditions Mean

North Chicago sits on Lake Michigan in Lake County and had 30,759 residents in the 2020 census. The lake shapes the weather, and Lake County averages about 36 inches of precipitation a year, with a January mean near 15 degrees Fahrenheit and a July mean near 82.

Those numbers explain the call pattern. Winter brings frozen and split supply lines, spring brings thaw and basement seepage, and summer brings humid air that slows drying and feeds mold. The Lake County hazard mitigation plan records flood events in 1996, 2000, 2004, 2013 and 2017, including April 2013, when heavy rain flooded areas outside mapped floodplains across northeastern Illinois.

Most of the city drains toward Lake Michigan, while the western part drains toward the North Branch of the Chicago River. The city operates its own water plant at 35 Lakeview Drive, with water drawn from the lake, and its Public Works office lists 847-596-8870 for weekday service and 847-596-8774 for after-hours emergencies.

Water Categories in Plain Words

The industry standard, ANSI/IICRC S500, sorts water by how contaminated it is. Category 1 is clean supply water, Category 2 carries some contamination such as washing machine overflow, and Category 3 is grossly contaminated, such as sewage or rising flood water.

The category decides what can be dried and what has to be removed. A carpet wet by a clean pipe leak can often be saved, while carpet that sat in sewage cannot. We identify the category before we tell you the scope.

What To Do in the First Few Minutes

Safe steps before a crew arrives

  • ✓Stop the source by closing the main water valve or the valve to the failed fixture.
  • ✓Stay out of standing water near outlets, cords and appliances, and switch off breakers only if the panel is dry.
  • ✓Lift valuables, papers and electronics onto dry surfaces.
  • ✓Photograph the damage, then call the 24/7 line.

If the damage is already a few days old, call anyway. Drying is still possible, and mold may be the next concern, which our mold remediation page explains.
