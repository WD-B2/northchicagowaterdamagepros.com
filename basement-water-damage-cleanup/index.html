Emergency Basement Water Damage: What To Do While Help Is On the Way

We take basement water calls 24/7. While you wait, do not go downstairs if water is near outlets, the furnace or the electrical panel, and call the utility if you need power cut from outside.

Basement safety

  • ✓Stay out of standing water until power to the area is confirmed off.
  • ✓Do not run a furnace or water heater that is sitting in water.
  • ✓If a sump pump has stopped and the power is off, do not carry a generator inside. Run it outdoors only.
  • ✓Carry out what you safely can from the top of the stairs, and photograph the waterline.

How We Clean Up a Finished or Unfinished Basement

Crews pump standing water, extract from carpet and flooring and remove ruined materials such as pad, insulation and the lower portion of drywall. Cutting wet drywall a foot or two above the waterline, flood cut, lets framing dry.

Dehumidifiers matter more here than in a living area, since basements hold humidity. Technicians set up equipment with daily checks, consistent with our structural drying work, and sanitize surfaces touched by contaminated water. Carpeted rooms may involve decisions covered in carpet water damage.

Waterproofing Is a Different Job

We clean up and dry water damage. We do not install permanent waterproofing systems, and a cleanup crew should not promise that water will never return. If water keeps coming in after the cleanup, the cause needs a separate fix such as grading, gutters, sump work or a waterproofing contractor.

We can tell you where moisture readings point and what we saw, so you can ask the right questions when you talk to a waterproofing specialist.

Insurance and Basement Water

Basement coverage depends on the cause. A burst pipe is commonly covered. Groundwater and rising flood water usually are not, and sewer or drain backup is covered only with an added endorsement, according to the Illinois Department of Insurance.

Photograph everything, keep receipts and hold on to damaged materials until your insurer has seen them. Your insurer decides coverage, and we document the work.
