Why Crawl Space Water Matters More Than It Looks

A crawl space is the lung of a house. Moisture here rises into subfloors, joists and the air you breathe, so a wet crawl space is a living-space problem even if you never go down there.

Standing water can also hide electrical and plumbing hazards. Stay out of it, and look from the access hatch if you want a first view. If the water is deep or the area has wiring, wait for the crew.

Standing water

Pooled water from rain, groundwater or a pipe break needs a pump and a look at the source.

Wet insulation

Insulation between joists soaks up water and holds it against the wood. It usually needs to come out.

Damp soil or liner

Wet soil and vapor barriers keep humidity high even after standing water is gone.

Musty smell upstairs

Odor coming through floors is often the first sign that crawl space moisture is spreading.

What the Crew Does

Technicians enter with protective gear, pump standing water and inspect framing, insulation and the vapor barrier. Moisture meters tell them how wet joists and the subfloor are.

Wet insulation and any damaged vapor barrier are removed, and dehumidifiers and air movers dry the space. Equipment setup is tighter in a crawl space, and technicians work lying down. The process follows the same standards as the structural drying work in the rest of the building.

When Mold or Rot Is Already There

If joists are dark, soft or fuzzy, stop and get them looked at. Sound wood can be dried and treated, but rotted framing needs a carpenter. Mold growth is handled with containment, as described on our mold remediation page.

The Illinois Department of Public Health notes that no standards exist to decide what amount of mold is acceptable, and it does not recommend testing as a first step. Fixing the moisture source and drying wet areas within 24 to 48 hours is the practical advice.

Insurance and Crawl Space Water

Sudden pipe failures that flood a crawl space are often covered. Groundwater, seepage and long-term dampness often are not. Check your policy and ask your insurer before authorizing large repairs.

Photograph the crawl space before removal, save receipts and keep the plumber's report if a pipe was the cause.

Crawl Spaces Along the Lake County Shore

Ground near Lake Michigan stays wet in spring, and Lake County's annual precipitation of about 36 inches keeps water tables high at times. Cold winters add frozen lines to the list of crawl space hazards.

We serve North Chicago and also respond in Winthrop Harbor and Fort Sheridan. If water reaches the main living level, see flood damage cleanup.
