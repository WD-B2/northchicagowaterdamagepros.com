We take water damage calls from Winthrop Harbor 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. While you wait, shut off the water source if you can, keep clear of standing water near outlets and call right away.

If Water Is Still Spreading in Winthrop Harbor

These steps limit damage in the first few minutes, before any crew is on site.

Stop the source

Close the main water valve or the valve to the failed fixture. If the source is outside, such as storm water, move people away from the wet area.

Protect power

Stay away from outlets and cords near standing water. Switch off breakers only if the panel is dry and easy to reach.

Lift what you can

Move electronics, papers and small furniture onto dry surfaces. Do not strain to move heavy pieces.

Document the damage

Take photographs and short videos of the water, damaged items and the source before cleanup begins.
