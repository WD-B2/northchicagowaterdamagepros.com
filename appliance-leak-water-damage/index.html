Emergency Appliance Leak: First Steps While You Wait

We take appliance leak calls 24/7. While you wait, shut the supply valve behind the appliance and unplug it only if the outlet is dry and you are not standing in water.

In the first few minutes

  • ✓Turn off the shutoff valve for the washer, dishwasher or icemaker line. If there is none, shut the main.
  • ✓Pull the appliance away from the wall only if you can do it without stepping in water.
  • ✓Sop up what you can with towels, and lift rugs and cords off the floor.
  • ✓Take photos, and note the appliance model and age for your insurance file.

How Hidden Water Shows Up

The sneaky appliance leak is slow. A refrigerator icemaker line can weep for months behind a panel, and a washer hose can seep onto a subfloor with no puddle at all.

Signs include a musty smell near the unit, cupped or soft flooring, a stain on a ceiling below and paint bubbling on nearby walls. Moisture meters and thermal imaging let a technician check without tearing anything open.

What the Crew Does After an Appliance Leak

The crew extracts standing water, tests the flooring and walls near the appliance and removes materials that cannot be dried. Cabinet kickplates and baseboards often come off so air movers can dry the cavity behind them.

Flooring decisions depend on the type. Laminate and engineered wood swell fast, while vinyl and tile often survive if the subfloor under them dries. See our pages on hardwood floor damage and carpet damage for those calls.

Appliance Leaks and Insurance

Sudden leaks from an appliance, such as a burst hose, are commonly covered for the water damage. Many policies exclude damage from continuous seepage over time. The distinction between sudden and gradual often decides the claim.

Keep the failed hose or part, photograph the model plate and write down when you first noticed the leak. We document the loss in writing, and your insurer decides coverage.

Where the Guidance Comes From

Our drying plans for appliance leaks follow ANSI/IICRC S500, the standard reference for water damage restoration. It treats clean supply-line water differently from washer or dishwasher discharge, which is why we ask what the appliance was doing when it failed.

Safety Steps Around a Leaking Appliance

Do these first

  • ✓Do not touch a plugged-in appliance while standing in water.
  • ✓Never reach behind a unit to unplug it if the floor is wet.
  • ✓Switch off the breaker for the area only if the panel is dry and easy to reach.
