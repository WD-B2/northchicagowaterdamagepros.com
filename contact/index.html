What to Have Ready When You Call

Helpful details

  • ✓Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • ✓Where the water is coming from, if you know, and whether it has stopped.
  • ✓Which rooms or floors are wet and roughly how deep the water is.
  • ✓Whether the water looks clean, dirty or like sewage.
  • ✓Whether you have shut off the water or power.

What We Will Tell You

We tell you what to do while you wait, such as where to shut off the water and what to move off the floor. We explain that scope and price are given before work starts and that our drying plans reference ANSI/IICRC S500, and we do not quote exact prices over the phone without understanding the loss.

For non-urgent questions about a past job, records or an estimate for scheduled work, email us and we will reply during normal business hours. If you are not sure which page applies, start at the services page.

Which Towns We Serve
