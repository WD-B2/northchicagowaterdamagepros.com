Bluffs, Ravines and Water

The neighborhood sits on a lakeside bluff cut by ravines. The Fort Sheridan Forest Preserve, run by the Lake County Forest Preserves, covers 317 acres with a 70-foot bluff overlook, six ravines and about three quarters of a mile of Lake Michigan shoreline.

That landscape matters for water damage. Runoff follows the slope toward the ravines and the lake, and a basement on a slope can take water from uphill after a heavy rain. Properties near the bluff edge also face wind-driven rain from the lake.

Insurance Near the Shore

Flood damage is generally excluded from homeowners policies, according to the Illinois Department of Insurance, and sewer backup requires added coverage. The department also notes you do not need to be in a floodplain to buy flood insurance, which usually takes effect 30 days after purchase.

Fort Sheridan History and What It Means for Your Property

Fort Sheridan was an Army post from 1887 to 1993, and the post was named in 1888. Its water tower, built in 1891 at 227 feet and lowered to 169 feet in 1940, still marks the skyline. Many properties here are part of a historic district, so ask the local jurisdiction whether any preservation review applies before exterior repairs.

For interior water damage, the practical steps are the same as anywhere else: stop the source, dry quickly and document. We also serve Winthrop Harbor to the north, and our home base is North Chicago.

If Water Is Still Spreading in Fort Sheridan

These steps limit damage in the first few minutes, before any crew is on site.

Stop the source

Close the main water valve or the valve to the failed fixture. If water is coming from outside, keep people away from the wet area.

Protect power

Stay away from outlets and cords near standing water. Switch off breakers only if the panel is dry and easy to reach.

Lift what you can

Move electronics, papers and small furniture onto dry surfaces. Leave heavy pieces for the crew.

Document the damage

Take photographs and short videos of the water, damaged items and the source before cleanup starts.
