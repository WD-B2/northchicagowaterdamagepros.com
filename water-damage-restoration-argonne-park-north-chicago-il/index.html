How Drying Works After a Leak

Standing water is extracted first, because pumps pull far more water in an hour than fans can evaporate in a day. The crew then removes materials that cannot be saved and sets air movers and dehumidifiers.

Technicians log moisture readings until the structure reaches dry standards. That approach follows ANSI/IICRC S500, the industry standard for professional water damage restoration, and you receive records at the end. See water extraction and structural drying for the details.

Insurance Notes and Records

If Water Is Still Spreading

A few minutes of careful action can keep a leak from getting larger.

Shut off the water

For a water heater or appliance leak, close the valve at the unit or the main shutoff. Turn off the heater only if the area around it is dry.

Keep power safe

A wet utility room can have wet outlets and a wet panel. If you cannot reach the breaker without stepping in water, leave it alone.

Move what you can

Lift rugs, boxes and electronics off damp floors if it is safe. Take photos of the damage before and after you move things.
