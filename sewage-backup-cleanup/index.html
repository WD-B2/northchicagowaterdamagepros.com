Emergency Sewage Backup: Stay Safe While Dispatch Is On the Way

We answer sewage backup calls 24/7. While you wait, stop using toilets, sinks, showers and the washer, because every gallon you flush can come back up through the lowest drain.

Protect people first

  • ✓Keep children, pets and anyone with a weak immune system out of the affected area.
  • ✓Do not wade in the water without waterproof boots and gloves, and wash hands well after any contact.
  • ✓Turn off the furnace fan or HVAC if the water is near vents or ducts.
  • ✓Do not try to clean with a household vacuum. It spreads contamination into the air.

Why Is Water Coming Up Through the Floor Drain?

A backup means wastewater cannot leave the home fast enough, so it takes the easiest path out, which is the lowest drain. Finding which of the three usual causes applies decides who needs to fix it.

CauseTypical signWho repairs it
Clog in the home's lateral lineBackup in one house only, often with roots or greasePlumber
Collapsed or broken sewer pipeRepeated backups, slow drains throughoutPlumber or excavation contractor
Overloaded municipal sewer after heavy rainNeighbors report the same problem after a stormReport to the city or sewer district
Failed sump or ejector pumpWater in basement with working main drainPlumber

Once the line is cleared, the contaminated water has to be removed and the room restored. The first call is often to a plumber, and the second is ours.

How We Clean Up After a Sewage Backup

Technicians wear protective gear and extract the contaminated water first. They then remove porous materials that absorbed it, including carpet and pad, drywall and insulation, and bag them for disposal.

Hard surfaces, framing and concrete are cleaned and treated with antimicrobial products, then dried with air movers and dehumidifiers. This follows the industry practice laid out in ANSI/IICRC S500, which treats sewage as grossly contaminated water. Contents that cannot be cleaned safely are removed with your approval.

Sewer Backup Coverage Is Often Optional

The Illinois Department of Insurance explains that sewer and drain backup is sometimes covered under a homeowners policy if that coverage has been added. Without it, a sewage loss may be excluded even though the damage is large.

Check your declarations page for water backup coverage, and ask whether a limit applies. Photograph the damage, keep receipts and save samples of removed materials if your adjuster requests them. Your insurer decides coverage, and we document the work in writing.

Do Not Treat Sewage Like Ordinary Water

The materials that can be dried after a clean supply line leak are often discarded after sewage. That includes carpet, pad and wet drywall. Families sometimes try to save these items because they look fine, and that is where later odor and illness risk comes from.

If other water is involved, see our pages on water extraction and flood damage cleanup. If the area has stayed damp for days, ask about mold remediation too.

Sewer Backups in North Chicago After Heavy Rain

Heavy rain can overload combined and separate sewer systems alike, and Lake County has seen repeated flood events in 1996, 2000, 2004, 2013 and 2017 according to its hazard plan. Basements and ground floors with floor drains are the first places water arrives.
