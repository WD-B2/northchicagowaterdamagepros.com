Emergency Flood Cleanup: Safety Steps While You Wait

Flood response runs 24/7 in North Chicago. While you wait, stay out of floodwater, because it can hide live electrical hazards and carry contamination that clean supply water does not.

Before anyone enters a flooded space

  • ✓Leave if water is near outlets, appliances or the electrical panel. Call your utility to shut power off from outside.
  • ✓Do not wade through floodwater without boots and gloves, and keep children and pets out entirely.
  • ✓Do not run a furnace, water heater or sump pump that is standing in water.
  • ✓Photograph the waterline and damaged items from a safe spot before anything is moved.

What Floodwater Is and Why It Matters

Restoration standards sort water into categories by how clean it is. Rain that enters through a window starts cleaner than water that rises through a floor drain, and both get worse the longer they sit.

ANSI/IICRC S500 describes three categories, from clean water to grossly contaminated. Floodwater that has crossed ground, a drain or a sewer line is generally treated as contaminated, so porous materials it touched are normally removed instead of dried.

Category 1Broken supply line, clean rain intrusionMost materials can be dried if action is quick
Category 2Dishwasher overflow, sump failure, washing machineCarpet pad and drywall often removed, hard materials cleaned
Category 3Sewage, rising river or groundwater, drain backupPorous materials removed and discarded, surfaces sanitized

How a Flood Cleanup Runs

The crew confirms the source, checks for electrical and structural hazards and starts bulk extraction. Contaminated materials are removed and bagged, then surfaces are cleaned and treated before drying begins.

Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the structure, and technicians log moisture readings until the building is back to target levels. The process matches the water extraction and structural drying work, with added cleaning steps for contaminated water.

Flood Insurance Is Not the Same as Homeowners Insurance

The Illinois Department of Insurance states that flood damage is typically excluded from standard homeowners policies. Flood coverage is usually a separate policy, often through the National Flood Insurance Program, and it generally takes effect 30 days after purchase.

You do not have to live in a mapped floodplain to buy it. If you are not sure what your policy covers, ask your agent about water backup, flood and surface water separately, because each is handled differently.
