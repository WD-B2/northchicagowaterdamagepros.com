Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and the third-party services this site loads. Last updated November 12, 2026.

24/7 Emergency Dispatch(847) 368-7353☎ Call Now

Information You Give Us

This site has no web forms. When you call (847) 368-7353 or email contact@northchicagowaterdamagepros.com, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, address and a description of the damage.

We use that information to respond to your request, arrange service and keep records of the work. We do not sell personal information.

Information Collected Automatically

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested. We use those logs for security and to keep the site running. This site does not set advertising cookies.

Third-Party Services on This Site

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and show maps from Google Maps. When your browser loads those resources, Google may receive technical information such as your IP address under its own privacy policy.

When you tap a phone number, your device starts a call through your phone carrier, which handles the call under its own policies.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep job records for as long as needed to support the work, answer questions from you or your insurer and meet legal and accounting requirements.

Your Choices

You can ask us to correct or delete personal information we hold about you, subject to legal and record-keeping requirements. Email contact@northchicagowaterdamagepros.com with your request.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy and will change the date at the top when we do. Continued use of the site means you accept the updated policy.
